Expanding

Recall the order of operations

BIDMAS

Consider

$x(x+1)$ $=x\times x $ $+\, x\times 1$

$\;\;\;=x^2 + x$

Distributive law

$a(b+c)$ $=a\times b $ $+ \,a\times c$

$\;\;\;\;=ab + bc$



Examples

$\quad =2(3x+4)$

Difference of Two Squares

$a^2 - b^2= $ $(a+b)(a-b)$

We can check this by expanding:

$(a+b)(a-b)$ $=a\times a $ $-\, a\times b $ $+\, b\times a $ $-\, b\times b $

$\quad =a^2 $ $-\, ab $ $+\, b a $ $-\, b^2 $

$\quad =a^2 $ $-\, ab $ $+\, a b $ $-\, b^2 $

$=a^2-b^2\qquad\;\; $
