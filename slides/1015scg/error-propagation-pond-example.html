Week 4 Task Question 6 - Find the error of the volume of the pond.

Recall that $V=\pi r^2h$. The formula for uncertainty is given by

$\ds \Delta V = \sqrt{\left(\frac{\partial V}{\partial r}\Delta r\right)^2+ \left(\frac{\partial V}{\partial h} \Delta h\right)^2}$

The partial derivatives are $\ds \frac{\partial V}{\partial r}= 2\pi r h\;$ and $\;\ds \frac{\partial V}{\partial h} = \pi r^2$

Since $r = 2$, $h=0.5$, $\Delta r = 0.05$ and $\Delta h =0.05,$ we have

$ \ds \frac{\partial V}{\partial r}= 2\pi (2)(0.5) = 2\pi\;$ and $\; \ds \frac{\partial V}{\partial h}= \pi (2)^2 = 2\pi$

So $ \ds \Delta V = \sqrt{\left(2\pi (0.05)\right)^2+ \left(4\pi(0.05)\right)^2} = \sqrt{0.01\pi^2+ 0.04\pi^2} =\pi\sqrt{0.05}\approx 0.70 $

Therefore $V = 6.28 \pm 0.70 \text{m}^3.$
