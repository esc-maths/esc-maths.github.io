The Goal of the Workshops

  • Provide opportunity to practice and review lecture content.
  • Teach you the skills necessary for completing other assessment items.
  • Give you a chance to chat with your demonstrator and each other about assessments.

Admin info

  • Workshops every 2 Weeks
  • You are expected to have attempted the workshop questions ahead of time. Most of our time is assigned to the workshop task.
  • Look through the FAQs under the “Learning Journey” page on Canvas.

Workshop Structure

  • Review of two previous Workshop problems ($\approx$ 10 mins)
  • Workshop task ($\approx$ 60-90 mins)
  • Rest of the time Workshop problems
  • Grading (last 10 mins)
