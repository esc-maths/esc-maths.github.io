Workshop 12


Activity: Review the article

Part I - Consider the clarity of the writing, the quality of the evidence provided, the logic of the arguments, and how the section connects to the broader literature.

  1. Does the introduction clearly set up the problem and research gap?
  2. Are the methods reproducible?
  3. Do the results align with the stated objectives?
  4. Does the discussion overstate the findings?

Part II - Check the editing of the paper, including clarity of figures, referencing, grammar, spelling, formatting, and any missing citations.

Time duration: 1hour 30 mins
