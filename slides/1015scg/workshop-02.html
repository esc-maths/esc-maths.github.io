Unit conversions (in scientific notation)

g) What is the average weight of a blood cell, in pounds?

Average mass of a (typical) red blood cell:

$\sim27\ \text{pg}=27\times10^{-12}\,$g.

Since $\ds 1 \text{ lb} = 453.59 \text{ g},$ then $\ds 1 =\frac{ 1 \text{ lb} }{453.59 \text{ g}}.$ Now we can convert to pounds:

\( \ds 27\times10^{-12}\ \text{g} \) \( \ds =27\times10^{-12}\ \text{g}\times \frac{ 1 \text{ lb} }{453.59 \text{ g}} \) \( \ds = \frac{27}{453.59} \times 10^{-12}\text{ lb} \qquad \qquad\;\;\;\; \)

\( =\ds 0.05953\times 10^{-12}\text{ lb} \) \( =\ds 5.953\times 10^{-2}\times 10^{-12}\text{ lb} \)

\( =\ds 5.953\times 10^{-14}\text{ g} \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad\;\; \)

b) Swimming pool chemistry - Dimensions: \( \ds 50 \text{m} \times 25 \text{ m} \times 3 \text{ m} \)

- Recommend hypochlorous acid $52.46\,$g/mol (molar mass)

- Concentration of $30\,$$\mu$mol/L.

We need to compute the mass knowing that $\,\text{Molar mass}(M) =\ds \frac{\text{mass}}{\text{num. of mols}}$

First, let's get the number of moles from the Molar concentration$\ds\left( \frac{\text{num. of moles}}{\text{Vol. of sol.}}\right)$

Pool volume = \( \ds 50 \text{m} \times 25 \text{ m} \times 3 \text{ m} \) \( \ds = 3750 \text{ m}^3 \) \( \ds = 3750000 \text{ L} \)

Molar concentration = $30\, \mu/\text{L}$ $=30 \times 10 ^{-6}\text{ mol}/\text{L}$

Num. of Moles required = $30 \times 10 ^{-6}\text{ mol}/\text{L} \times 3750000 \text{ L}$ $=112.5 \text{ mol}$

b) Swimming pool chemistry - Dimensions: \( \ds 50 \text{m} \times 25 \text{ m} \times 3 \text{ m} \)

- Recommend hypochlorous acid $52.46\,$g/mol (molar mass)

- Concentration of $30\,$$\mu$mol/L.

We need to compute the mass knowing that $\,\text{Molar mass}(M) =\ds \frac{\text{mass}}{\text{num. of mols}}$ 👈

Num. of Moles required = $30 \times 10 ^{-6}\text{ mol}/\text{L} \times 3750000 \text{ L}$ $=112.5 \text{ mol}$

$\text{mass} = \text{molar mass} \times \text{numb. of mols} \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad $

$=52.46 \text{ g/mol}\times 112.5 \text{ mol}$ $=5901.75 \text{ g}$ $=5.90175 \text{ kg}$

What is the minimum amount of HClO (in kg) that has to be added to the pool, when the pool is refilled after it has been drained? Answer: $=5.90175 \text{ kg}$
