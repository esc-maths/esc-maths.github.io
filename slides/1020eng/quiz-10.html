Quiz 10


Question

Consider the ODE $\,y' -2y = -4x + 2.$

  1. Find the general solution of the homogeneous differential equation $\,y' -2y =0.$
  2. Solve the ODE with the initial condition $y(0)=1.$




Solution Question 1.

First solve the homogeneous equation: \[ y' - 2y = 0. \]

This is a separable equation: \[ \frac{dy}{dx} = 2y. \]

Integrating, \[ \ln |y| = 2x + C. \]

Thus the homogeneous solution is \[ y_H = C e^{2x}. \]

Solution Question 1.

Write the ODE in standard linear form: \[ y' + 4xy = 2x. \]

The integrating factor is

\(\ds I(x) = \exp\left(\int 4x\,dx\right) \) \( = e^{2x^2}. \)




Solution Question 2. Multiply the ODE by the integrating factor:

\( e^{2x^2}y' + 4x e^{2x^2}y = 2x e^{2x^2}. \)

The left-hand side is a product derivative: \( \ds\frac{d}{dx}\left(y e^{2x^2}\right) = 2x e^{2x^2}. \)

Integrate both sides: \(\;\ds y e^{2x^2} = \int 2x e^{2x^2}\,dx \) \(\ds = \frac{1}{2} e^{2x^2} + C. \)

Solve for \(y\): \( \ds\;y(x) = \frac{1}{2} + C e^{-2x^2}.\; \) Apply the initial condition \(y(0)=5\):

\( 5 = \ds\frac{1}{2} + C\) \(\Rightarrow C = \ds\frac{9}{2}. \)

Final solution: \( \ds y(x) = \frac{1}{2} + \frac{9}{2} e^{-2x^2}. \)
