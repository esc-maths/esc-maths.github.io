Question 1

Evaluate the following limits:

  1. $\ds\lim_{x\to 3} \ln\left(e^{3x}\right)$
  2. $\ds\lim_{x\to 4} \frac{x^2-16}{x-4}$

Question 2

Find the value(s) of $k$ that makes the following function continuous over the given interval

\( f(x) = \left\{ \begin{array}{cc} 3x+2 & x\lt k \\ 2x - 3 & k\leq x\leq 8 \end{array} \right. \)



Solution Question 1

Evaluate the following limits:

  1. $\ds\lim_{x\to 3} \ln\left(e^{3x}\right)$ $=\ds\lim_{x\to 3} 3x$ $=3(3)$ $=9$
  2. $\ds\lim_{x\to 4} \frac{x^2-16}{x-4} $ $=\ds\lim_{x\to 4} \frac{(x-4)(x+4)}{x-4}$ $=\ds\lim_{x\to 4} (x+4)$ $=8$

Solution Question 2

\( f(x) = \left\{ \begin{array}{cc} 3x+2 & x\lt k \\ 2x - 3 & k\leq x\leq 8 \end{array} \right. \)

First, \(\ds\lim_{x\to k^-} = 3k+2\,\) and \(\,\ds\lim_{x\to k^+} = 2k-3.\) Then \(3k+2= 2k-3.\)

Hence $k=-5.$
