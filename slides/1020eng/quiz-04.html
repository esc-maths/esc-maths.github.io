Question 1

Determine the intervals where the graphs of the following functions are concave upwards and where they are concave downward

  1. $f(x)= x^2-x+12$
  2. $f(x) = x^3 +15x^2+6x+1$

Question 2

The height $h(t)$ (in metres) of a ball thrown vertically upward is given by \[h(t) = 20 - 5t^2\]

  1. Find the velocity of the ball at time .
  2. Determine the time at which the ball reaches its maximum height.


Solution Question 1

  1. $f(x)= x^2-x+12$ $\Ra f'(x) = 2x-1\,$ and $\,f''(x) = 2$
    Since the second derivative is always positive, the graph is always concave upward and there are no points of inflection.
  2. $f(x) = x^3 +15x^2+6x+1$ $\Ra f'(x) = 3x^2+30x+6\,$ and $\,f''(x) = 6x+30$
    When $x\gt -5$ the graph is concave upwards, when $x\lt -5$ the graph is concave downwards. There is a point of inflection where the concavity changes at $(-5,221).$

Solution Question 2

  1. If $h(t) = 20 - 5t^2$, then velocity is $\,v(t) = h'(t) $ $= 20 - 10t$.
  2. Max height: We must solve $\,20 - 10t = 0,$ that is $t= 2$ s.
