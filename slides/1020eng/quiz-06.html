Question 1

Evaluate the integral $\ds \int_1^2 \left(x^2+1\right)\, dx.$



Question 2

Use integration by substitution to evaluate the integral $\ds \int_0^1 \left(x+1\right)^9\, dx$



Solution Question 1

Consider the function $F(x) = \ds\frac{x^3}{3} + x$. Notice that $F'(x) = x^2+1.$ Thus

\(\ds \int_1^2 \left(x^2+1\right)\, dx \) \(=\ds \left[\frac{x^3}{3} + x\right]_1^{2}\) \( \ds = \left[\frac{(2)^3}{3} + (2)\right] - \left[\frac{(1)^3}{3} + (1) \right]\qquad\qquad\)

\( \ds =\left[\frac{8}{3} + 2\right] - \left[\frac{1}{3} + 1\right] \) \( \ds = \frac{7}{3} + 1 \) \( \ds = \frac{10}{3} .\)





Solution Question 2

Set $u = x+1,$ then $du = dx.$ Also $\underbrace{u = 0+1=1}_{\text{lower bound}}$ and $\overbrace{u = 1 + 1 =2}^{\text{upper bound}}.$ Then

$\ds \int_0^1 \left(x+1\right)^9\, dx$ \( \ds= \int_1^2 u ^9 \, du\) \( \ds = \left[\frac{u^{10}}{10}\right]_1^{2} \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad\qquad\)

\( \ds = \frac{(2)^{10}}{10} -\frac{(1)^{10}}{10} \) \( \ds = \frac{1023}{10} \) \( \ds =102.3 \)
