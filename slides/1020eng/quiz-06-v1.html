Question 1

Evaluate the following integral: $\ds \int_0^3 \left(x+1\right)^4\, dx.$



Question 2

Find the value of $k$ such that: $\ds \int_0^k x^2\, dx = 9.$



Solution Question 1

Consider the function $F(x) = \ds\frac{1}{5}(x+1)^5$. Notice that $F'(x) = \left(x+1\right)^4.$ Thus

\(\ds \int_0^3 \left(x+1\right)^4\, dx =\ds \left[\frac{1}{5}(x+1)^5\right]_0^{3}\) \( \ds = \frac{1}{5}(\mathbf 3+1)^5 - \frac{1}{5}(\mathbf 0+1)^5 \qquad\quad\)

\( \ds = \frac{4^5}{5} - \frac{1^5}{5} \) \( \ds = \frac{1024}{5} - \frac{1}{5} \) \( \ds = \frac{1023}{5} .\)





Solution Question 2

Find the value of $k$ such that: $\ds \int_0^k x^2\, dx = 9.$

Now consider $F(x) = \ds \frac{x^3}{3}.$ Notice that $F'(x) = x^2.$ Thus

$\ds \int_0^k x^2\, dx =\ds \left[\frac{x^3}{3}\right]_0^{k}$ \( \ds = \frac{k^3}{3} - \frac{0^3}{3}\) \( \ds = \frac{k^3}{3} \) \( \ds = 9 \)

From equation $\ds \frac{k^3}{3}=9$ we solve for $k.$ That is \( k^3 = 27.\) Therefore \( k = 9.\)
