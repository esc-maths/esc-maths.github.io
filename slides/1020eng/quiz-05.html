Quiz 5


Question 1

  1. Evaluate the following limit using L'Hopital's rule: \( \ds \;\lim_{x\to 0}\frac{\sin(5x)}{x}. \)
  2. Use the second-order Taylor polynomials for $\cos(x)$ around $x=0$ to prove that \(\ds \lim_{x\to 0} \frac{1-\cos(x)}{x^2}= \frac{1}{2}.\)

Question 2

The radius of a circular metal disk is measured to be 2 cm. If the radius increases by 0.02 cm,

  1. estimate the corresponding increase in the disk area,
  2. estimate the new area of the disk.


Solution Question 1

  1. As $x\to 0,$ $\sin(5x)\to 0$ and $x\to 0$. Using L'Hopital's rule we have that

    $\ds \;\lim_{x\to 0}\frac{\sin(5x)}{x}$ \(\ds =\lim_{x\to 0}\frac{5\cos(5x)}{1}\) \(\ds =\lim_{x\to 0}5\cos(5(0))\) \(\ds =5.\)


  2. We must prove that \(\ds \lim_{x\to 0} \frac{1-\cos(x)}{x^2}= \frac{1}{2}.\)

    The second order Taylor polynomial of $\cos(x)$ is $1-\dfrac{x^2}{2}.$

    Then \(\ds\; \lim_{x\to 0} \frac{1-\cos(x)}{x^2} \) \(\ds =\lim_{x\to 0} \frac{1-\left(1-\dfrac{x^2}{2}\right)}{x^2}\) \(\ds =\lim_{x\to 0} \frac{\dfrac{x^2}{2}}{x^2}\) \(\ds=\lim_{x\to 0} \frac{1}{2}\) \(\ds=\frac{1}{2}.\)


Solution Question 2: Given radius $r = 2$ cm and $\Delta r = 0.02$ cm.

  1. Estimate the corresponding increase in the disk area:

    Since \(\,\ds \frac{\Delta A}{\Delta r} \approx \frac{dA}{dr}\,\) then $\,\ds \Delta A \approx \frac{dA}{dr}\ \Delta r.$

    Also, since $\,A = \pi r^2\,$ then $ \,\ds \frac{dA}{dr}\ = 2\pi r.$

    Thus \(\Delta A \approx 2\pi r \Delta r\) \(=\ds 2\pi (2)(0.02) \) \(=\ds \frac{2\pi}{25} \) \(\approx \ds 0.25 \)

  2. Estimate the new area of the disk:

    Compute \(\,A + \Delta A\) \(= \pi(2)^2 + \dfrac{2\pi}{25}\) \(= 4\pi + \dfrac{2\pi}{25}\) \(= \dfrac{102\pi}{25}\) \(\approx 12.81\)
