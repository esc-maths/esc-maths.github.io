Question 1

A thin rod lies along the \(x\)-axis from \(x=0\) to \(x=3,\) with density

\( \rho(x) = 2 + x^2 \;\text{kg/m}. \)

Find the mass of the rod.

Solution Question 1

A thin rod lies along the \(x\)-axis from \(x=0\) to \(x=3\). The density is \[ \rho(x) = 2 + x^2 \;\text{kg/m}. \]

The mass of the rod is given by \( \ds m = \int_0^3 (2 + x^2)\, dx. \)

\( m = \left[ 2x + \dfrac{x^3}{3} \right]_0^3 \) \(= \left( 2(3) + \dfrac{3^3}{3} \right) - 0 \) \( =6 + 9. \)

Therefore, the mass of the rod is \( m = 15 \;\text{kg}. \)

Solution Question Find the area between the curves \( f(x) = x^3 \) and \( g(x) = 4x. \)

That is: \( x = -2,\; x = 0,\; x = 2. \)

On \( [-2, 0] ,\) the curve \( f(x) \) lies above \( g(x) \). The area between the curves is

\( \ds A_1 = \int_{-2}^{0} \big( f(x) - g(x) \big)\, dx \) \(\ds = \int_{-2}^{0} \left( x^3 - 4x \right) dx \)

\( = \ds \left[ \frac{x^4}{4} - 2x^2 \right]_{-2}^{0} \) \( = 0 - (-4) = 4. \qquad\; \)

On \( [0, 2] ,\) the curve \( g(x) \) lies above \( f(x) \). The area between the curves is

\( \ds A_2 = \int_{0}^{2} \big( g(x) - f(x) \big)\, dx \) \( \ds = \int_{0}^{2} \left( 4x - x^3 \right) dx \) \( = \ds \left[ 2x^2 - \frac{x^4}{4} \right]_{0}^{2} \) \( = 4 \)

Therefore, the total area between the curves is \( \ds A = A_1 + A_2 \) \(= 4 + 4 = 8.\)
