Solution Question 1

A thin rod lies along the \(x\)-axis from \(x=0\) to \(x=3\). The density is \[ \rho(x) = 2 + x^2 \;\text{kg/m}. \]

The mass of the rod is given by \( \ds m = \int_0^3 (2 + x^2)\, dx. \)

\( m = \left[ 2x + \dfrac{x^3}{3} \right]_0^3 \) \(= \left( 2(3) + \dfrac{3^3}{3} \right) - 0 \) \( =6 + 9. \)

Therefore, the mass of the rod is \( m = 15 \;\text{kg}. \)

Comparing coefficients gives the system: \(A+B=1,\; 2A=1.\)

This implies \( A = \dfrac12 \) and \( B = \dfrac12 \).
