Problem 2

The matrix

\( A = \begin{pmatrix} \cos \theta & -\sin \theta \\ \sin \theta & \cos \theta \end{pmatrix} \)

represents a counterclockwise rotation by angle \(\theta\) about the origin in \(\mathbb{R}^2\).

  1. Describe the steps to perform a rotation about an arbitrary point \(P = (x_0, y_0)\) instead of the origin.
  2. Use homogeneous coordinates to construct a matrix that performs a rotation by angle \(\theta\) about an arbitrary point \(P = (x_0, y_0)\).


Problem 4

Let $T$ be an affine transformation in $\mathbb R^n.$

  1. Show that $T$ is not linear.
  2. If $\mathbf x, \mathbf y, \mathbf z\in \mathbb R^n,$ show that $T$ obeys the law \[ T(\mathbf x- \mathbf y+\mathbf z) = T(\mathbf x)- T(\mathbf y)+T(\mathbf z) . \]
