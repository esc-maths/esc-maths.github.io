Question 1

What are the modulus and argument of $\dfrac{2}{3-i}$?

Solution: Multiply numerator and denominator by the conjugate of the denominator: $$\frac{2}{3-i} \cdot \frac{3+i}{3+i} = \frac{2(3+i)}{3^2 + 1^2} = \frac{6+2i}{10} = \frac{3}{5} + \frac{1}{5}i.$$ Modulus: $\left|\frac{3}{5} + \frac{1}{5}i\right| = \sqrt{\left(\frac{3}{5}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{1}{5}\right)^2} = \sqrt{\frac{10}{25}} = \frac{\sqrt{10}}{5}.$

Argument: $\arg\left(\frac{3}{5} + \frac{1}{5}i\right) = \tan^{-1}\frac{1/5}{3/5} = \tan^{-1}\frac{1}{3}.$


Question 2

Express all possible values of $1^{4/3}$ in Cartesian form.

Solution: In complex form, $1 = e^{i 2 k \pi}$ for $k \in \mathbb{Z}$. Then $$1^{4/3} = \left(e^{i 2 k \pi}\right)^{4/3} = e^{i \frac{8 k \pi}{3}}.$$ For $k = 0,1,2$ (distinct values modulo $2\pi$):

  • $k=0$: $e^{i 0} = 1$
  • $k=1$: $e^{i 8\pi/3} = e^{i 2\pi/3} = -\frac{1}{2} + i\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
  • $k=2$: $e^{i 16\pi/3} = e^{i 4\pi/3} = -\frac{1}{2} - i\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$

Question 3

What is the value of $\ds \lim_{z \to \infty} \frac{2z^3-1}{z^2+1}$?

Solution: Divide numerator and denominator by $z^2$: $$\frac{2z^3-1}{z^2+1} = \frac{2 z - \frac{1}{z^2}}{1 + \frac{1}{z^2}}.$$ As $z \to \infty$, $\frac{1}{z^2} \to 0$, so the limit becomes $$\lim_{z\to\infty} \frac{2z - 0}{1 + 0} = \infty.$$

Question 4

Show that the function $f(z)=e^y e^{ix}$ is nowhere analytic.

Solution: Let $f(z) = u(x,y) + i v(x,y)$.

Here $f(z) = e^y (\cos x + i \sin x)$, so $u = e^y \cos x$, $v = e^y \sin x$. Cauchy-Riemann equations require $u_x = v_y$ and $u_y = -v_x$. Compute derivatives: $$u_x = -e^y \sin x, \quad v_y = e^y \cos x,$$ $$u_y = e^y \cos x, \quad -v_x = - e^y \cos x.$$ Since $u_x \neq v_y$ for any $(x,y)$, the Cauchy-Riemann equations are never satisfied. Hence, $f(z)$ is nowhere analytic.


Question 5

Let $C$ be the contour of the circle $|z|=2$ from $z_1=2$ to $z_2=2i$ in the first quadrant. Find an upper bound for $$I = \int_C \frac{dz}{z^2-1}.$$

Solution: Use the ML-inequality: $\left|\int_C f(z) dz \right| \le \max_{z\in C} |f(z)| \cdot L$, where $L$ is the arc length. On $|z|=2$, $|z^2-1| \ge |2^2 - 1| = 3$, so $\max |f(z)| = \frac{1}{3}$. Arc length of first quadrant quarter-circle: $L = \frac{\pi}{2} \cdot 2 = \pi$. Hence, upper bound:

$\ds |I| \le \frac{1}{3} \cdot \pi = \frac{\pi}{3}.$
