Boids: Flocking Simulation

Boids is a computer simulation that models the flocking behaviour of birds using a few simple rules. Each boid follows its own movement while responding to nearby boids.

  • Separation: Avoid getting too close to neighbouring boids.
  • Alignment: Adjust direction to match nearby boids.
  • Cohesion: Move towards the centre of nearby boids.

Together, these simple rules produce complex, collective movement without a central leader.
