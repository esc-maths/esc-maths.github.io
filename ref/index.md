# Some notes to navigate through the GU system

## Assessment mapping

Assessment mapping is the first step for a course entry within 
the Grade Management System.

It is recommended that assessment items are set up at the 
beginning of the trimester. But you can do it at the end. 
Once the Course Profile is published and the assessment 
items are set up in your Canvas course site, the assessment
mapping should be completed within the Grade Management System. 

In portal page:
1. Learning and Teaching 
2. Faculty Centre
3. Grade management and support
4. Recommend Grades

## Grades

Once marks have been finalised in Canvas Markbook, proceed to
the Grade Management System. In portal page:
1. Learning and Teaching 
2. Faculty Centre
3. Grade management and support
4. Recommend Grades
5. View "Courses"
